## Understanding Open, Closed, and Filtered Ports

Understanding common port states makes scan results easier to interpret.

- **Open:** A service appears to be accepting connections on the scanned port.
- **Closed:** The target is reachable, but no service is accepting connections on that port at the time of the scan.
- **Filtered:** A firewall or another network control may be preventing the scanner from determining whether the port is open or closed.
- **Unresponsive:** The target may be offline, unreachable, or configured not to respond to the type of probe being used.

Different scanning methods and applications may report these states differently. Network conditions, firewall rules, and service configurations can affect the results.
